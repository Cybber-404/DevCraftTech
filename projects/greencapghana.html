Overview

A credible front door for a bigger story

GreenCap Ghana is a purpose-driven agricultural enterprise based in the Savannah Region, using veterinary-led, climate-smart goat farming as its flagship initiative — with dairy, leather, veterinary services, farmer training, and consultancy planned as the model scales. The site needed to make that full picture legible in a single scroll, for an audience of investors, NGOs, and government partners rather than everyday consumers.

Challenge

Not simply a goat farm

The central messaging challenge was positioning: GreenCap's flagship activity is goat farming, but the business is a broader rural-development model. The site needed to lead with that flagship (it's the proof point) without letting visitors mistake the whole enterprise for a single livestock operation.

The audience also mattered more than usual. This isn't a retail storefront — it's a document investors, NGOs, and government agencies use to decide whether a conversation is worth having, so credibility, clarity, and a real partnership CTA had to carry more weight than typical marketing polish.

Research & Planning

Structuring the investor journey before the visuals

Rather than a typical marketing homepage flow, the page was structured around the questions a funding partner actually asks in order: what is this, what problem does it solve, why this team, what's the model, what's the roadmap, who's behind it, how do I reach you.

Design Process

One long page, built to feel like several

A single-page long-scroll layout keeps the whole story in one continuous narrative instead of forcing partners to click through separate pages. Scroll-triggered reveals (data-reveal) and a looping farm video in the hero give the page rhythm without turning it into a slideshow.

Development

Plain, fast, and easy to hand off

Built in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no framework overhead — appropriate for a single-page marketing site that the client can maintain without a build step. The contact form is wired to EmailJS with a dedicated HTML email template, so partnership inquiries land directly in GreenCap's inbox.

A pre-deployment audit caught two real issues before they reached production: several image and video filenames contained raw spaces (fragile across hosts and tooling), and the Open Graph share image referenced a file that didn't exist. Both were fixed — filenames normalized and references updated, and the social preview image pointed at the logo mark until a dedicated cover photo is ready.

Lessons Learned

A deploy that "works" isn't the same as one that's verified

Even a simple static site can fail in production in ways that never show up locally — filenames with spaces, a missing social image, or a domain alias silently pointing at the wrong deployment. Treating "it's deployed" and "it's verified" as two separate steps caught every one of those before they reached a real visitor.
