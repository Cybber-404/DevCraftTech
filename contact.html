FAQ

Common questions

How quickly will I hear back?

Most messages get a reply within one business day. If your project is time-sensitive, mention that in your message and we'll prioritize accordingly.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes — we work with clients across the region and beyond, communicating asynchronously and over calls as needed.

What information should I include in my message?

A short description of what you're building, your rough timeline, and any budget range you have in mind. The project type and budget fields in the form above are optional but help us reply faster.

Can you take over an existing project?

Absolutely. Share a link or a short summary of the current codebase and we'll let you know what a handover would look like.
